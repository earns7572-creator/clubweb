# Speaker rebuild validation findings

Model Lab verification on 2026-08-19 found that the rebuilt HIGH cabinet now reads as a compact horn-only enclosure from front, three-quarter, and side angles, with no exposed compression-driver cylinder. FULL has removed the prior separate hanging acoustic assembly, but its front horn/woofer composition still needs a flatter recessed-mouth treatment so the front silhouette does not read as a large pointed dark shape in active light.

The active FULL dark validation also showed that the current horn/driver emissive surfaces remain too broad. The next refinement must keep horn and cabinet albedo neutral, move the color to small throat/cone emitter cores, and retain only the already-added short local radial halo at those emitters.

After replacing the cone-shaped front element with a flat recessed cone and converting horn mouths to cabinet-contained recesses, the MODEL LAB three-quarter views no longer show a floating triangular horn element. FULL now reads as a tapered two-way cabinet with an upper framed horn, a single recessed woofer, and two small ports; HIGH reads as a compact horn-only enclosure. Active dark checks show red, yellow, and green at the emitter cores rather than colored cabinet albedo. The low-light Model Lab is intentionally severe, so the final production POV check remains necessary for local-halo strength.
